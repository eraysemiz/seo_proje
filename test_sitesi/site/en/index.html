Natural Black Tea and Herbal Infusions

Çaylıbahçe is a small tea shop based in Trabzon, on the Black Sea coast of Türkiye. We work directly with a dozen family growers in the misty hills of the eastern Black Sea region and pack our teas in small batches, so every pouch reaches you while the leaves are still fresh and fragrant.

Our range starts with classic Turkish black tea, brewed the traditional way in a double teapot, and extends to delicate first flush leaves, spiced winter blends and herbal infusions gathered on the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts. Each product page explains the water temperature, steeping time and storage tips that bring out the best in the leaves.

How we work

During the harvest season we visit the gardens and plan the picking dates together with our growers. The leaves are withered, rolled and dried in small workshops rather than large factories, then packed in our own warehouse every week. This takes more effort, but it means we always know which garden and which harvest each pouch comes from.

We avoid plastic packaging wherever we can and use recyclable paper and cardboard instead. Every order includes a small card with brewing suggestions, so that even first time tea drinkers can make a balanced cup at home.

Brewing Turkish tea

Turkish tea is brewed in two stacked pots. Water boils in the larger kettle below while the tea steeps in the smaller pot on top for twelve to fifteen minutes. To serve, pour a little of the strong tea into a tulip shaped glass and top it up with hot water until you reach the colour you like. Lighter cups are called açık, stronger ones koyu.

Shipping

At the moment we ship within Türkiye only. If you would like to order from abroad, please write to us and we will let you know when international shipping becomes available. We are happy to answer questions about our teas in English.
